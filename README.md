# Kang Taewook (Woogi)

I am a product engineer who builds and stabilizes production systems where mobile apps, connected devices, AI agents, and automation meet.

My career started in Flutter multi-platform product engineering and expanded into AX/AI agent engineering. I focus on work that goes beyond 0-to-1 delivery: production rollout, field operations, release automation, monitoring, incident response, and continuous stabilization.

I also work across planning, design, operations, support, and marketing workflows to find team bottlenecks, automate repetitive work, and help products ship reliably with smaller teams.

## What I Deliver

- AX engineering: Claude Code, Codex CLI, Gemini CLI, OpenClaw, and Multica harnesses with reusable workflows, agents, skills, slash commands, and team templates.
- Product shipping: cross-functional automation for planning, design, operations, support, marketing, release, monitoring, and stabilization.
- AI/RAG products: vector DB, PromptOps, prompt versioning, prompt engineering, MCP, tool calling, LangChain/LangGraph, Gemini, Vertex AI embeddings, semantic routing, SSE streaming, structured output, and evaluation harnesses.
- Flutter product leadership: mobile, desktop, TV, smart glasses, BLE central/peripheral, kiosk mode, OTA, and field device operations.
- Full-stack execution: Next.js, React, FastAPI, Python, PostgreSQL, Redis, Firebase, and GCP Cloud Run.
- Automation systems: Slack/Discord agents, Playwright, uiautomator2, HTTP crawlers, Google Sheets workflows, KakaoTalk operation automation, and SNS lead-generation pipelines.
